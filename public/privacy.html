Alphabite — Privacy Policy

Alphabite does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your game progress — per-character practice statistics, best scores, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere and is deleted when you delete the app (or clear site data in a browser).

What we don't do

No accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No third-party SDKs that collect data. The app makes no network requests to our servers, because we don't have any.

Speech

Character pronunciations are generated on your device by the system text-to-speech engine. No audio is recorded.
